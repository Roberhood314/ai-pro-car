AI PRO CAR – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

AI PRO CAR is an independent automotive diagnostics and technical-assistance application. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information we may process

Vehicle profile data you enter, diagnostic codes, symptoms, maintenance records, uploaded vehicle images, and limited Pi account information only when you explicitly authorize Pi authentication.

Pi Network data

AI PRO CAR only receives Pi account data that you explicitly consent to share through Pi Network. We do not claim access to unrelated Pi account information.

Use of information

Information is used to provide vehicle diagnostics, maintenance history, technical guidance, account access, security, and app functionality.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by infrastructure or service providers necessary to operate the app, subject to applicable permissions and agreements.

Vehicle and image data

Vehicle information and uploaded images are used only for diagnostic and technical-assistance workflows. Real AI image analysis may require an authorized backend service.

Security

AI PRO CAR uses reasonable technical safeguards. Sensitive automotive operations remain in Safe Read mode unless a separately authorized integration is available.

Your choices

You may choose not to provide optional data. Pi permissions are controlled through the Pi consent flow.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the AI PRO CAR developer through the official app listing or developer channel.
